Robots · 02

Two robots, two personalities.

Axe and Echo both generate what they say through an AI speech pipeline in real time — not a list of pre-written lines. Same underlying build, different temperament.

Axe

▂▄▆▄▂

"Tell me what you need — I'll get it done."

The direct one. Axe is built for quick tasks and voice commands, and answers in as few words as the task allows. Every reply is generated live through an AI speech pipeline, so the exact phrasing changes each time — nothing is recorded or scripted.

Speech AIPythonSensorsAutomation

Echo

▃▅▇▅▃

"I'm here, and I'm listening."

The listener. Echo holds context across a whole conversation instead of just executing single commands, and responds with a slower, warmer cadence — built to feel like company, not just a tool.
